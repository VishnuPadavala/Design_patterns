
**Adapter Design Pattern**

The Adapter Design Pattern is a Structural Design Pattern.

__Problem:-__

Sometimes we have an existing class, but its interface is not compatible with what the client expects.

Think of:

__Mobile charger needs USB-C__
__Old charger provides Micro USB__

You cannot connect them directly.

__An Adapter acts as a bridge between them.__

same that the Adapter acts as a bridge between them to act comfortable to the conditions
